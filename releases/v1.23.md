- The Imperial Star Destroyer has a sharper wedge hull, layered armour, a rebuilt bridge and engine bank, and a recessed hangar for TIE launches.
- Hyperspace arrivals begin with a distant point of light. A streak grows toward the arriving ship, then the distant point catches up and the streak collapses into the ship.
- Departing ships leave a streak along their path. Once the ship vanishes, the streak retracts from the launch point toward the distant light. The arrival and departure cameras are framed to show the length of the effect.

Requires an Apple Silicon Mac running macOS 13 or newer. The app and disk image are signed and notarized.
