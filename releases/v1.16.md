Adds arena Ace history and the selected Asteroid Duel artwork as the app icon.

- The HUD shows how many distinct pilots have held Ace and the longest completed-encounter survival streak. Records survive pilot losses and reset with a new arena.
- Finder, the Dock and the launched app use the same bundled icon, with standard and Retina sizes.
- Release disk images include the app icon and matching mounted-volume artwork.
- Local builds regenerate icons automatically from the supplied source image before signing.

Apple Silicon only, macOS 13 or newer. Building, signing and notarization run locally.
