- A ringworld arcs across the sky, with procedural terrain on its inner surface.
- A black hole bends the starfield around an animated accretion disk.
- A fleet graveyard scatters torn hulls through the asteroid belt. Pilots avoid the wrecks just as they avoid asteroids.
- All three join the regular sector rotation and can appear when you press **T**.

Requires an Apple Silicon Mac running macOS 13 or newer. The app and disk image are signed and notarized.
