Adds veteran squadrons, ace pilots, an infinite galaxy skybox, and steadier flight.

- Squadrons surviving two enemy encounters receive new pilots for their lost ships before the next enemy faction arrives. Surviving pilots keep their damage and history.
- Pilots surviving four encounters become aces, marked by gold, camera-facing scratch tallies above their ships. Every fifth stroke crosses the previous four.
- The finite star-particle shell is replaced by a seeded, infinitely distant skybox with varied stars, Milky Way arm clouds, dark dust lanes and a distant spiral galaxy.
- Pilot commands smooth small steering corrections, ignore sub-degree hunting, and damp banking. Urgent collision avoidance stays responsive.
- Release builds, signing, notarization and packaging all run locally. There are no GitHub Actions workflows or cloud signing keys.

The Universal DMG and ZIP support Apple Silicon and Intel Macs running macOS 13 or newer. The app and DMG are Developer ID-signed, notarized and stapled. Use `SHA256SUMS` to check the downloads. Drag the app into Applications; quitting or closing its window releases its keep-awake assertions.
