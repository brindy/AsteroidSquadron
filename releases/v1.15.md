Makes Ace a unique, earned title and moves all new builds to Apple Silicon only.

- At most one living pilot across both factions can be Ace. It requires at least three survived encounters and six personal enemy kills; the highest-kill eligible pilot holds the title.
- Ties retain the incumbent. A higher-scoring eligible pilot takes the title, and a dead Ace is replaced by the next eligible leader or leaves the title vacant.
- Only the Ace carries gold scratch marks. Three completed encounters plus the current one starts the tally at four; it advances with each further survived encounter.
- Lethal weapon hits record their actual firing pilot and generation. Nonlethal hits, environmental destruction, and replacement pilots do not receive false kill credit.
- New app builds, DMGs and ZIPs contain only arm64 and require an Apple Silicon Mac running macOS 13 or newer.

Building, signing, notarization and packaging run locally. GitHub hosts source and explicitly published releases only. The app and DMG are Developer ID-signed, notarized and stapled; `SHA256SUMS` verifies the files. Quitting or closing the app releases its keep-awake assertions.

Unofficial personal fan project. Star Wars, Battlestar Galactica and Babylon 5 names and designs belong to their respective owners.
