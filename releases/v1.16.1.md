Refines the new app icon's Dock sizing and includes arena Ace history.

- Fits the selected Asteroid Duel artwork into a centered 824px footprint on the 1024px traditional macOS icon canvas, with transparent margins at every standard and Retina size.
- Finder, the Dock and the mounted DMG share the same artwork.
- The HUD records distinct Ace pilots and the longest completed-encounter survival streak in the current arena. Records survive pilot losses and reset with a new arena.

Apple Silicon only, macOS 13 or newer. Building, signing and notarization run locally.
