Stargate joins the asteroid belt. F-302s launch from the Daedalus, Death Gliders from a Goa’uld mothership, and Darts from a Wraith hive ship.

- Atlantis arrives beyond the belt. Squadrons divert for close passes against its shield and hull while 30 Ancient drones stream from the central spire. The camera follows the attack from the tower, outer piers, and fighters.
- Survivors patrol the belt after Atlantis is destroyed. The city can return after at least ten more encounters.
- Fighters without opponents fly long patrol routes through and around the asteroids.
- The HUD shows the current Ace’s kills and encounters survived. The title stays vacant until an eligible pilot beats the previous record. A gold star marks the current Ace.

Requires an Apple Silicon Mac running macOS 13 or newer. The app and disk image are signed and notarized.
