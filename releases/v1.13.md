An endless cinematic crossover dogfight in a procedurally generated asteroid field.

- Six factions: X-wings, TIE fighters, Colonial Vipers, Cylon Raiders, Babylon 5 Starfuries, and Shadow fighters.
- Formation arrivals, immense capital ships, blink FTL, and swirling blue-black jump windows with proper hull clipping.
- Starfury drift-and-pivot attacks, physical forward gunfire, predictive collision avoidance, asteroid impacts, and turbulent explosions.
- Wide battle coverage, critical-moment cuts, continuous launch pullbacks, and incoming-squadron handoffs.
- Procedural planets with four biomes and drifting volumetric gas.
- Built-in keep-awake while the app runs; quit or close its window to release it.

Download the **Universal DMG** for Apple Silicon and Intel Macs running **macOS 13 or newer**. Open it, drag **Asteroid Squadron** into **Applications**, then launch it. The ZIP contains the same app as an alternative. Both formats contain a Developer ID-signed, notarized app; the DMG is also notarized. `SHA256SUMS` lists the download checksums.

Drag the scene to move the window; double-click to fill or restore it. **A** toggles cinematic cameras, **Space** pauses, **R** generates a fresh arena, **B** changes the planet biome, **K** toggles keep-awake, and **⌘Q** quits. Press **?** for all controls.
