- Choose a local soundtrack to time automatic camera cuts and some arrivals to the music. Major cinematics keep priority.
- The waveform shows playback progress. Use its × button to remove the track, or press **R** to restart the arena and the music together.

Requires an Apple Silicon Mac running macOS 13 or newer. The app and disk image are signed and notarized.
