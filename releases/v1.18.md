Capital ships and Atlantis now arrive against the limb of a much more distant planet. The camera follows fighters out of the hangar and brings the action into focus, with softer backgrounds on close shots and more of the battle in focus on wide shots.

- Atlantis waits for one faction to win and receive reinforcements. At low shield strength, that faction’s capital ship arrives, launches another top-up, and opens fire.
- Atlantis launches 108 Ancient drones from the central tower. They make three passes through the capital ship’s hull as the camera follows the counterstrike.
- The next rival faction arrives after the siege ends and the survivors have regrouped in the asteroid belt.

Requires an Apple Silicon Mac running macOS 13 or newer. The app and disk image are signed and notarized.
